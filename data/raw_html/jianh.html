Jian Huang

Associate Professor
(217) 300-3104
jianh@illinois.edu
212 Coordinated Science Laboratory

  • Jian's research homepage

Education

  • Georgia Institute of Technology; Ph.D., Computer Science; August 2017.

Academic Positions

  • Associate Professor, Electrical and Computer Engineering, 100% Appointment, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, August 2024 - present

Professional Highlights

  • [Research] As of April 2026, I have published 60+ papers at a variety of the strongest computer architecture and systems conferences in my field, including ISCA, MICRO, ASPLOS, HPCA, OSDI,SOSP, PLDI, FAST, EuroSys, and USENIX ATC, and a dozen patents issued worldwide. Some of my research have been transferred into industry products or received IP license requests. The students graduated from my group joined top places in both academic and industry, including tenure-track Assistant Professor in ECE@Purdue University, research scientist at Meta, and software/hardware engineers at NVIDIA, Apple, AMD, and Airbnb.
  • [Teaching] My research has had an impact on our graduate/undergraduate education. I have been developing an undergraduate-level course on AI Systems and Engineering. I developed the first graduate-level course on memory and storage systems at UIUC, and the course has been approved as a permanent course ECE522: Emerging Memory/Storage System. I also fully revised the graduate-level course ECE511: Computer Architecture in Fall 2022 by employing the most recent computer architecture topics. I will teach a new course titled ECE498 JH: AI Systems and Engineering in Fall 2026.
  • [Awards] My research has received some significant awards, including inaugural ACM SIGMICROEarly Career Award, NSF CAREER Award, NSF CRII Award, Y. T. Lo Faculty Fellow in ECE a tUIUC, Dean’s Award for Early Innovation, NVMW Memorable Paper Award Finalist, MICRO Best Paper Runner Up, MICRO Best Artifact Award, MICRO Hall of Fame, IEEE Micro Top Picks (three times), IEEE Micro Top Picks Honorable Mention, USENIX Best Paper Award, Google Faculty Research Award, NetApp Faculty Fellowship Award, Microsoft Research Outstanding Project Award, and Best Dissertation Award in the College of Computing at Georgia Tech.
  • [Grants] As of April 2026, I have received over $4.5M grants ($12.32M in total with my collaborators) from NSF, DOE, DARPA, Army Research Office, IBM-Illinois Discovery Accelerator Institute, and industry companies. I am currently advising 9 Ph.D. students, 6 master students, and multiple undergraduate students.
  • [Media Coverage] My research on AI infrastructure and non-volatile memory/storage systems have been featured in popular press. For instance, our recent work on energy-efficient NPUs and inter-core connected AI chips were reported by Semiconductor Engineering and Semi IP Hub.Our secure storage research received 30+ media reports that include ACM CACM, ACMTechNews, TechXplore, Science Daily, HIT Infrastructure, Cybersecurity Insiders, etc; our wearable storage research received 50+ media reports that include The Register, CACM, TechTimes, NeoWin, ZDNet, OnMSFT, etc.
  • [Services] I constantly serve on the PC of architecture and systems conferences such as ISCA, MICRO,HPCA, ASPLOS, FAST, and EuroSys, and as reviewers for journals such as CAL, TACO,TOC, TOS, and TPDS. I was invited to serve as the program co-chair of the 14th Annual Non-Volatile Memory Workshop (NVMW’23) which usually has more than 200 attendees worldwide. I am an associate editor of the journal IEEE Computer Architecture Letters, and the founder of the Workshop on Hot Topics in System Infrastructure (HotInfra).
